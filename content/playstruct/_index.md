### INTRODUCTION

PlayStruct Junior is a modular, construction-based furniture system intentionally designed for kids aged 5–7. It transforms the traditionally passive act of assembling furniture into a vibrant journey of building, discovery, and active interaction.

Featuring over 4+ core archetypes and 10+ customizable configurations, PlayStruct Junior grows with your child’s imagination. It’s not just about giving them a place to sit or draw, it's about inviting them to co-create their own environment, turning everyday furniture into an interactive playground of cognitive development and spatial discovery.

### CONCEPT

How do we bridge the gap between static utility and dynamic imagination? Our journey began by completely dismantling and decoding two everyday concepts: "furniture" and "play."

We realized that furniture doesn't have to be a finished product delivered to a room—it can be a starting point. Somewhere along the line, the breakthrough happened: we shifted our focus from the final object to the act of assembly itself. For a child aged 5–7, the process of putting things together, testing boundaries, and having creative autonomy over their own space is the ultimate form of play.
